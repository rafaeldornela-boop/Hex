# Audio Assets & Licenses — Hexagon World (Hex)

All audio assets under `public/audio/` are original instrumental compositions and procedural soundscapes created specifically for Hexagon World (Hex), free of commercial copyright restrictions.

## Original Compositions & Foley
- **Author:** Hexagon World Project (procedural MIDI compositions & foley synthesis)
- **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (Public Domain Dedication)
- **Music Tracks (`public/audio/music/`):**
  - `normal.mp3` — *Alvorada do Reino* (D Dorian medieval pastoral: acoustic lute/nylon guitar, orchestral harp, flute, recorder, cello, string ensemble)
  - `prosperity.mp3` — *Feira Dourada* (G Mixolydian/Major medieval town theme: acoustic guitar, harp, flute, oboe, pizzicato strings, tambourine)
  - `crisis.mp3` — *Inverno Escuro* (D Minor somber tension: cello, slow strings, oboe, sparse harp, muted timpani)
  - `war.mp3` — *Estandartes de Ferro* (E Minor martial strategy: French horn, strings, cello ostinato, timpani, war drums)
  - `discovery.mp3` — *Horizontes Antigos* (F Lydian wondrous exploration: orchestral harp, celestial strings, flute, celesta, cello)
- **Ambient Soundscapes (`public/audio/ambient/`):**
  - `forest.mp3`, `water.mp3`, `city.mp3`, `mining.mp3`, `port.mp3`, `rain.mp3`, `storm.mp3`, `night.mp3`
- **Acoustic Sound Effects (`public/audio/sfx/`):**
